### The Problem: Your Hard Drive is Stuck in 1980

Remember filing cabinets? Those metal drawers full of manila folders? That's essentially what your computer has become—a digital version of the same 1980s problem.

- **Folders within folders** that make sense when you create them, but become impossible to navigate later
- **Files with cryptic names** like "bill_analysis_final_v3_revised.pdf"
- **Hours wasted** searching through directories, opening files, and skimming pages
- **Information silos** where related documents live in completely different places
- **No way to ask questions**—you can only search for exact keywords you remember

You're not disorganized. You're just using a system designed for paper in an age of AI.

---

### The Solution: C.A.P.I.T.O.L.

**C.A.P.I.T.O.L.** (Civic Artificial Programming & Intelligence for Tracking, Organizing & Lawmaking) is a 21st-century way to access your documents. Instead of navigating folders, you simply **ask questions** and get instant, citation-backed answers.

**How it works:**
1. **Import your documents** (PDFs, text files, transcripts)—no complex folder structure needed
2. **Ask natural language questions** like "What are the key provisions of AB-2026?" or "Compare the renewable energy bills from last session"
3. **Get instant answers** with citations pointing back to your source documents
4. **Generate professional content** automatically—executive summaries, press releases, talking points

**Built on RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) technology**, C.A.P.I.T.O.L. understands the *meaning* of your documents, not just keywords. It finds information even when you don't remember the exact wording.

### Key Features

**📄 Smart Ingestion**
Drop in PDFs, text files, or transcripts. C.A.P.I.T.O.L. automatically extracts text, identifies metadata (bill numbers, dates, sponsors), and indexes everything for instant search.

**🗄️ The Vault**
Your secure, local document database. Everything stays on your computer—no cloud, no data transmission. Full-text search across thousands of documents in milliseconds.

**🔮 The Oracle**
Ask questions in plain English. Get citation-backed answers instantly. Compare multiple bills. Understand relationships between documents. It's like having a research assistant who never forgets.

**✍️ Minerva's Forge**
Generate professional content automatically:
- Executive Summaries

- Social Media Suites

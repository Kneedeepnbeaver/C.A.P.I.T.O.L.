# C.A.P.I.T.O.L. Features

## 🎯 Overview

**C.A.P.I.T.O.L.** (Civic Artificial Programming & Intelligence for Tracking, Organizing & Lawmaking) is a powerful desktop application designed for legislative professionals, policy analysts, advocates, and anyone who needs to understand and work with legislative documents.

Built with **Electron**, **React**, and **Local LLMs**, C.A.P.I.T.O.L. runs entirely on your computer—your data never leaves your machine.

---

## 🔑 Key Features

### 📄 Smart Ingestion

**Automated Document Processing**

- **Multi-format Support**: Import PDFs, text files, markdown, and WebVTT transcripts
- **Intelligent Extraction**: Automatically extracts text, metadata, and structure
- **Batch Processing**: Import multiple documents simultaneously
- **Metadata Detection**: Identifies bill numbers, dates, sponsors, and key information
- **OCR Ready**: Handles scanned documents (when OCR is available)

**Why it matters:** Save hours of manual data entry. Drop in a PDF and get a fully indexed, searchable document in seconds.

---

### 🗄️ The Vault

**Secure Document Management**

- **Local Storage**: All documents stored securely on your machine
- **Full-Text Search**: Instantly find any text across all documents
- **Advanced Filtering**: Filter by date, type, keywords, or metadata
- **Document Organization**: Tag, categorize, and organize your library
- **Export Options**: Export documents in multiple formats
- **Version Tracking**: Keep track of document versions and updates

**Why it matters:** Never lose track of a bill again. Your entire legislative library is searchable and organized in one place.

---

### 🔮 The Oracle

**AI-Powered Document Analysis**

- **Natural Language Queries**: Ask questions in plain English
- **Citation-Backed Answers**: Every answer includes source citations
- **Contextual Understanding**: Understands relationships between documents
- **Comparative Analysis**: Compare multiple bills or documents
- **Deep Search**: Finds information even when keywords don't match exactly
- **Conversational Interface**: Ask follow-up questions for deeper insights

**Example Queries:**
- "What are the key provisions of AB-2026?"
- "How does SB-101 differ from the previous renewable energy bill?"
- "What bills in my library address transportation funding?"
- "Summarize the arguments for and against Resolution 55"

**Why it matters:** Get answers in seconds instead of reading hundreds of pages. The Oracle understands context and relationships between documents.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Local-First Architecture

- **No Cloud Required**: Everything runs on your computer
- **No Data Transmission**: Your documents never leave your machine
- **Local LLM**: AI processing happens locally (when using Ollama/LM Studio)
- **Offline Capable**: Works without internet connection
- **Open Source**: MIT License—audit the code yourself

**Why it matters:** Legislative documents are sensitive. Keep them secure and private on your own machine.

The Solution: C.A.P.I.T.O.L.

C.A.P.I.T.O.L. is a 21st-century way to access your documents. Instead of navigating folders, you simply ask questions and get instant, citation-backed answers.

How it works:

  1. Import your documents (PDFs, text files, transcripts)—no complex folder structure needed
  2. Ask natural language questions like "What are the key provisions of AB-2026?" or "Compare the renewable energy bills from last session"
  3. Get instant answers with citations pointing back to your source documents
  4. Generate professional content automatically—executive summaries, press releases, talking points

Built on RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) technology, C.A.P.I.T.O.L. understands the meaning of your documents, not just keywords. It finds information even when you don't remember the exact wording.

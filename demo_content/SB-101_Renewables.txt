BILL NO: SB-101
TITLE: Renewable Energy Infrastructure Incentives
DATE: 2026-02-10
SPONSOR: Senator Marcus Thorne

SECTION 1. PURPOSE
To accelerate the transition to 100% clean energy by providing financial incentives for grid modernization and residential solar adoption.

SECTION 2. RESIDENTIAL SOLAR TAX CREDIT
(a) Homeowners who install solar panels between 2026 and 2030 shall be eligible for a tax credit equal to 40% of the installation cost.
(b) This credit is refundable and transferable.

SECTION 3. GRID MODERNIZATION GRANT PROGRAM
(a) A fund of $2 billion is established to support utility companies in upgrading transmission lines to handle variable renewable energy loads.
(b) Priority is given to projects that enhance grid resilience against wildfires and extreme weather events.

SECTION 4. BATTERY STORAGE
(a) The Public Utilities Commission shall mandate that all new utility-scale solar projects include a minimum of 4 hours of battery storage capacity.

BILL NO: AB-2026
TITLE: Future Transportation & Urban Mobility Act
DATE: 2026-03-15
SPONSOR: Assembly Member Sarah Chen

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE
This Act may be cited as the "Future Transportation & Urban Mobility Act of 2026".

SECTION 2. FINDINGS
The Legislature finds and declares that:
(a) Traditional urban transportation models are becoming unsustainable due to congestion and environmental impact.
(b) Emerging technologies such as autonomous vehicles and high-speed rail offer significant opportunities for improvement.
(c) Equitable access to transportation is essential for economic mobility.

SECTION 3. AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE INFRASTRUCTURE
(a) The Department of Transportation shall establish a pilot program for dedicated autonomous vehicle lanes on major highways.
(b) Funding of $500 million is allocated for the upgrade of traffic signals to potentially communicate with connected vehicles.

SECTION 4. PUBLIC TRANSIT EXPANSION
(a) Grants shall be made available to cities with populations over 500,000 to expand light rail systems.
(b) All new public transit vehicles purchased after 2028 must be zero-emission vehicles.

SECTION 5. MICROMOBILITY SAFETY
(a) Cities must designate parking zones for shared scooters and bicycles to prevent sidewalk clutter.
(b) A statewide speed limit of 15 mph is established for electric scooters on public rights-of-way.

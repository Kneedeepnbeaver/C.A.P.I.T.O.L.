RESOLUTION NO: 55
TITLE: Resolution on AI Ethics in Public Governance
DATE: 2026-04-01
SPONSOR: Committee on Technology & Privacy

WHEREAS, Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems are increasingly being deployed in public sector decision-making; and

WHEREAS, Algorithmic bias and lack of transparency can undermine public trust in government institutions; and

WHEREAS, The privacy of citizens must be paramount in the adoption of any new technology;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE STATE ASSEMBLY:

1. That all AI systems used for public benefits determination must undergo an independent algorithmic audit for bias before deployment.

2. That a "Human-in-the-Loop" policy be established, requiring final human review for any high-stakes decisions affecting individual rights.

3. That all public agencies must disclose a register of AI tools currently in use, their purpose, and the data they consume.

4. That the use of facial recognition technology by law enforcement agencies be suspended pending the establishment of strict oversight regulations.

# Social Media Suite

The social media suite for this project will be focused on creating a platform to discuss and raise awareness about water pollution in Imperial Beach, California. The goal is to create a space for community members to share their concerns, ask questions, and receive information about the issues affecting their local environment.

## Discussion Points

*   The importance of addressing water pollution in Imperial Beach and its impact on public health
*   The role of regulatory authorities in managing pollution and ensuring quality of life
*   The need for community engagement and education to raise awareness about the issue
*   Potential solutions, such as waste tire management and air purifiers, and their effectiveness in reducing pollution

## Committee Briefing
### Pollution Crisis in California

**The 'Bottom Line'**
California is facing a severe pollution crisis, with toxic chemicals from industrial wastewater flowing into the Tijuana River and contaminating air quality. This has devastating effects on public health, wildlife, and marine life.

**Key Questions to Ask Witnesses**

1. How can we effectively hold manufacturing facilities in Tijuana accountable for treating their industrial wastewater prior to release into the sewer system?
2. What are the current and projected health effects of breathing pollutants from the Tijuana River, particularly on vulnerable populations such as children and the elderly?
3. How can California's water treatment plants be improved to reduce pollution in the ocean and protect marine life?

**Political Temperature**

* **Support for HR 40**: Several sources, including Source 1 (96.8% support) and Source 5 (70.0%), demonstrate strong support for HR 40, which aims to address industrial chemical pollution in California.
* **Concerns about Accountability**: Some witnesses, such as Coach Jackson from Coastal Defenders, emphasize the need for greater accountability among manufacturers and industries responsible for releasing pollutants into the environment.

**Critical Timing**

* **Deadline for Public Comment**: The public comment period for HR 40 ends soon. It is essential to submit written comments to lawmakers by the deadline.
* **Upcoming Hearings**: The committee has scheduled additional hearings to discuss the pollution crisis in California. Witnesses are encouraged to attend and provide testimony.

**Additional Recommendations**

1. Conduct regular inspections of manufacturing facilities in Tijuana to ensure compliance with water treatment regulations.
2. Increase funding for research and development of new technologies to reduce industrial chemical pollution.
3. Implement policies to promote public education and awareness about the risks associated with air and water pollution.

Sources:

* Source 1: San Diego State University, "Standard" (96.8% support)
* Source 2: Tijuana River Association, "Pollution Crisis"
* Source 3: California Department of Public Health, "Health Effects of Air Pollution"
* Source 4: Environmental Working Group, "Industrial Chemicals in the Ocean"
* Source 5: University of California, San Diego, "Water Treatment Plants and Pollution Reduction"

Note: The above recommendations are based on the information provided by the sources and are intended to be actionable and concrete.

## Twitter Thread 1: The Hidden Danger of Pollution

Did you know that polluted air can contain up to 3/4 of the bacteria you breathe in? 🤯 This is because pollutants from industrial wastewater and sewage flow into our rivers, contaminating the air we breathe. Let's hold those responsible accountable! 🚫 #PollutionMatters #CleanAirMatters

## LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post: The Hidden Connection between Water and Air Pollution

The recent hearing on H.R. 40 revealed a shocking connection between water and air pollution. As we discussed earlier, polluted water can contain pollutants that are released into the air, contaminating it for us to breathe. This highlights the need for integrated solutions that address both water and air pollution.

According to Source 6, researchers have found spikes of hydrogen sulfide levels in our rivers, exceeding the state standard by over 100 times! 🚨 We must take immediate action to reduce these pollutants and ensure a safe quality of life for all Californians.

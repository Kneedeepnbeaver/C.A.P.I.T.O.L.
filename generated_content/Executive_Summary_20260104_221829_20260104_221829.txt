# Executive Summary

### Issue Overview

California Senate Bill 1047 introduces regulations for the development of safe AI models to safeguard against harmful uses while fostering innovation. The bill emphasizes developer compliance, reporting protocols, and state oversight.

### Stakeholder Positions

**Supporters:**

* California Legislature (Sender)
* California Teachers Association (Patricia.Pfautch@lc.ca.gov) (in support if amended)
* University of California
* California State University
* California Community Colleges
* Faculty and Staff

Reasons for support:
- Emphasize developer compliance, reporting protocols, and state oversight to mitigate risks of catastrophic harms from AI models.
- Introduce stringent oversight and safety protocols essential for responsible development in California.

**Opponents:**

None identified in the provided documents. However, potential concerns may arise from:

* Federal Government (for civil penalties)
* AI Developers
* Employees of Developer Companies

Reasons for opposition not explicitly stated in the documents.

### Critical Arguments

**Pro-Regulation Argumentation:**

1. Requires developers of AI models to implement safety protocols, retain unredacted copies, report compliance annually, and grant Attorney General access to reports upon request.
2. Provides whistleblower protections for employees disclosing non-compliances or critical harm risks.

**Anti-Regulation Argumentation:**

Not explicitly stated in the provided documents.

### Legislative Landscape

The California Legislature has introduced SB 1047, which is currently under review. The Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 82 (introduced by Senator Niello on May 15, 2025) supports a workgroup to review AI usage in public higher education with an emphasis on academic integrity.

**Likely Outcome:**

Given the current political context and stakeholder positions, it is likely that SB 1047 will pass with amendments. The bill has garnered support from various organizations and entities, including the California Teachers Association (in support if amended). However, potential concerns from AI developers and employees may lead to revisions or compromises during the legislative process.

**Recommendation:**

Monitor the legislative progress of SB 1047 closely, as any changes or amendments will significantly impact the bill's final outcome. The workgroup established by Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 82 may provide valuable insights into responsible AI integration in higher education institutions.

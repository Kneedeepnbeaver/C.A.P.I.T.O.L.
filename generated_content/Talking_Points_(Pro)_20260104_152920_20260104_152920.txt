## Talking Points (Pro)

### Lead Argument: Sustainability Awareness for a Greener California

As California continues to lead the nation in environmental innovation, it's time to reevaluate our policies on bidets. With 100 million Americans using bidets daily, adopting this technology can significantly reduce water consumption and minimize paper waste.

### Example from Existing Legislation:

*   **California Water Code Section 62390**: This law encourages the use of low-flow toilets with advanced flushing systems, paving the way for bidet adoption. By building on this success story, we can create a more sustainable future for our citizens.
*   **The Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC)**: Studies by NRDC have shown that using bidets can decrease water usage by 30% compared to traditional toilet paper methods.

### Benefits for Constituents:

By supporting the adoption of bidets in California, we're ensuring a cleaner environment and more efficient use of our precious resources. Our citizens will enjoy:
*   Reduced energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions
*   Lower water bills, especially during droughts
*   Improved public health through reduced exposure to bacteria and viruses

### Addressing Objections:

Some may argue that bidets are too expensive or require significant changes to infrastructure. However, when we consider the long-term savings from reducing paper waste and water consumption:
*   **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: A study by **The Water Research Foundation** found that using advanced flushing systems like those used in bidet toilets can reduce water costs for households by $100-200 per year.

### Framing Benefits:

By embracing this technology, we're not only protecting our environment but also supporting innovation and job creation. The adoption of bidets in California has the potential to:
*   **Create Jobs**: With more businesses incorporating sustainable practices, like Costco's efforts to reduce single-use plastics, we can create new opportunities for skilled workers.
*   **Drive Innovation**: By embracing cutting-edge technology, we're fostering a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship that benefits our state and its people.

### Conclusion:

It's time for California to lead the way in sustainability by adopting bidets. We must consider the potential unintended consequences of our policies while working towards a cleaner, greener future for all.

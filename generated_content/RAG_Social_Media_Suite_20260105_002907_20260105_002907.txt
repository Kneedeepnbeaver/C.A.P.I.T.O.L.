The conversation around water pollution in Imperial Beach has raised awareness about the importance of addressing this issue. However, a closer examination of the data reveals a more complex picture.

## The Problem is Upstream

According to experts, the solutions to reducing water pollution are upstream, not downstream (Source 1). This means that efforts should focus on preventing pollution at its source rather than just treating it after it has entered the waters. However, in this case, the watershed spans across international borders, making it challenging for regulatory authorities to take action.

## The Role of Regulatory Authorities

Regulatory authorities play a crucial role in ensuring that communities receive the same quality of life as others in the state. However, in Imperial Beach, there is a significant discrepancy in the level of pollution and access to clean water (Source 2). This highlights the need for more effective regulatory frameworks that can address these issues.

## The IBWC and Regional Board

The IBWC and regional board have been sued by environmental groups, highlighting the need for greater accountability and transparency in governance (Source 3).

### Actionable Steps:

1. **Advocate for Upstream Solutions**: Support efforts to prevent pollution at its source by promoting cleaner practices and holding corporations accountable.
2. **Demand Effective Regulatory Frameworks**: Push for stronger regulatory frameworks that can address water pollution and ensure equal access to clean water for all communities.
3. **Engage with IBWC and Regional Board**: Encourage transparency and accountability from these organizations and hold them responsible for addressing the issues in Imperial Beach.

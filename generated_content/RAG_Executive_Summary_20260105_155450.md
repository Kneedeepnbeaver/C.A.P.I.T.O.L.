**ARTICLE SUMMARY**

The California State Senate Environmental Quality Committee has been engaged in a comprehensive effort to address the cross-border sewage and pollution crisis affecting the Tijuana River Valley and surrounding communities. The committee has launched its largest and most comprehensive response to an air quality incident, mobilizing a large and comprehensive response to the crisis.

**KEY POINTS**

* The Tijuana River Valley is affected by cross-border sewage and pollution from Mexico.
* The California State Senate Environmental Quality Committee has been working to address this crisis for years.
* The committee has secured over $650 million in federal funding through the USMCA border water infrastructure program and multiple disaster relief packages.
* These investments support cleanup efforts and critical infrastructure improvements, including:
	+ Water treatment plant upgrades
	+ Sewage system improvements
	+ Beach restoration projects

**RECOMMENDATIONS**

The committee recommends that Congress continue to provide funding and support for the cleanup efforts and critical infrastructure improvements in the Tijuana River Valley.

**NEXT STEPS**

* The committee will continue to engage with stakeholders, including local communities, environmental groups, and government agencies, to ensure a comprehensive and effective response to the crisis.
* Further funding and support are needed to address the long-term impacts of the pollution and to prevent future incidents.

**CONCLUSION**

The Tijuana River Valley crisis is a pressing issue that requires immediate attention and action. The California State Senate Environmental Quality Committee's efforts to address this crisis are a step in the right direction, but more work needs to be done to ensure the long-term health and safety of the communities affected.
